# Demo script (frozen)

Taken from the hero demo in the [priority plan](../Cortex%20Core%20Priority%20Plan%20-%20Flawless%20Demo.md), which is the source of truth. **Frozen:** change it only through an issue labeled `area:demo` and agreed by the whole team. Rehearse until it runs the same every time. The person met in the demo is **Ash** (the plan calls them Priya; see [RISKS.md](RISKS.md), item 8).

Before going on stage: memory is seeded with earlier days of data (`make seed`), the brief and Ash answers are pre-warmed, and the backup video is on the laptop.

| # | Beat | Time | What happens | Checklist ids |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Meet | 20 s | Wearing the glasses, say "This is Ash from Acme." A contact card appears on the live dashboard with the moment attached. | D-1, D-3, D-8 |
| 2 | Promise | 20 s | Ash says "I'll send you the deck Friday." A confirm chip appears; one tap adds it to Waiting on, with the source moment. | D-4, D-8 |
| 3 | Ask with proof | 30 s | Ask "What did Ash promise me?" Cortex answers by voice and opens a proof receipt showing the exact moment. | D-2, D-5 |
| 4 | Brief | 20 s | "Brief me on Ash." Cortex speaks a 20-second brief: who Ash is, what you discussed, what is open. | D-6 |
| 5 | Act | 30 s | Cortex proposes a follow-up email. Approve it; it sends, and a receipt with undo appears. | D-7 |
| 6 | Call finale | 60 s | FaceTime Cortex from an iPhone. Hold up a business card or whiteboard to the camera; Cortex saves it to memory. Ask "What is open with Ash?" and Cortex answers from memory, showing the proof card on video if video back works (CALL-4). | D-10 |

Privacy (D-9) is shown on stage: pause or off-the-record, and delete a moment, visible to judges.

**Pitch line before the demo (rehearse it):** Meta AI answers about what you see right now; Cortex remembers across days, shows which moment an answer came from, and follows through with actions you approve.

## Fallbacks on stage

See the fallbacks table in the [priority plan](../Cortex%20Core%20Priority%20Plan%20-%20Flawless%20Demo.md): phone capture if the glasses fail, a second verified phone or the recorded call if Chert fails, hotspot for wifi, confirm-card correction for misheard names, cached answers if slow, a teammate plays Ash.
