# Product summary

Short pointer. The full PRD is the source of truth: [Project Cortex - Product Concept for HackUTA 2026.md](../Project%20Cortex%20-%20Product%20Concept%20for%20HackUTA%202026.md).

- **What:** a second brain for Ray-Ban Meta Gen 1 glasses plus a companion dashboard. It remembers what the wearer sees and hears, answers with proof (the source moment), and takes follow-up actions the user approves.
- **Modes:** Focus, Student, Life and Assist are presets over one shared memory. Memory is never split by mode.
- **Skills that lead the demo:** Network (log a person by introduction), waiting-on tracker, pre-meeting brief.
- **Action loop:** notice, propose, confirm, do, prove (receipt with source moment and undo).
- **Call layer:** Chert bridges FaceTime into a LiveKit room our own agent joins, so the same backend answers calls, sees the caller's camera and can show video back.
- **Output:** Gen 1 glasses have no display, so output is audio plus the dashboard.
- **Trust:** pause, off-the-record and delete everywhere.
- **Goal:** win the HackUTA 2026 main track and the Chert Best Use of FaceTime track with one flawless 3-minute demo ([DEMO_SCRIPT.md](DEMO_SCRIPT.md)).
